PLANNED / FUTURE MEDIA TOOLS

What we are building toward.

These concepts are not available yet. The focus is explainable checks that help people review media—not automated claims of certainty.

Upcoming tool concepts

PLANNED / NOT YET AVAILABLE

VIDEO / SYNTHETIC MEDIA

AI-generated video review

Examine frame-to-frame consistency, visual artifacts, and editing traces. Findings would need human review and source context.

AUDIO / SPEECH

Morphed audio & voice review

Look for possible voice cloning, speech synthesis, splicing, and other audio edits without treating a score as proof.

FACE / IDENTITY IMAGES

Face morph & swap review

Inspect possible identity blending, face replacement, and editing artifacts in portraits and identity photos.

IMAGE / SYNTHETIC MEDIA

AI-generated image review

Explore image-level clues and provenance signals for synthetic or heavily altered images, with clear limits on detection.

AUDIO + VIDEO

Lip-sync & media consistency

Compare speech timing, mouth motion, and audio-video alignment to surface possible mismatches for closer inspection.

PROVENANCE / CONTEXT

Content credentials viewer

Read available signed provenance and edit history, while distinguishing verifiable records from missing or stripped metadata.

A missing credential or detector match is not, by itself, evidence that media is fake.
