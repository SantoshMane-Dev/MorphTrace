A NOTE ON EVIDENCE & PRIVACY

Tools for review.
Not a verdict.

Image processing can surface useful leads, but ordinary editing, resizing, recompression, and camera pipelines can also affect forensic signals. Treat every result in context and corroborate it independently.

Image comparison runs locally in your browser. Converter and resize uploads are processed in memory; the metadata viewer uses temporary request storage. Forensic reviews keep uploaded images and generated evidence in the configured workspace. Avoid sharing sensitive images unless you trust the deployment you are using.
